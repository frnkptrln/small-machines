# Credits

**Small Machines** is the shared project and artist name. SID/65, PAULA/68, ACID/16, DRUM/08 and WARM/06 identify the instruments heard in a piece.

Frank Peterlein shaped the project through musical direction, listening, selection, naming decisions and iterative feedback. The instruments, compositions, rendering tools and documentation were developed with ChatGPT. Claude is collaborating on the visual treatments and music videos.

This repository makes the production process inspectable through scores, source code, parameters, generators and reports. Exported video files have not yet been supplied to this import; the video section contains timing data and a handoff for making them.

The built-in sound banks were synthesized for the project. They do not contain manufacturer ROMs or sampled commercial recordings. SID, Paula and Roland hardware informed parts of the instrument design; the implementations are original approximations. The technical history records design references and limitations.

For a music-video end card, the project name, piece title and actual instrumental lineup are the primary credits. The description or accompanying production notes can explain the contributions in more detail. This credit convention records the collaboration; it does not assign a new license to the material.
